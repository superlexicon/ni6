KYC Providers Have Your Full PII

When you verify on any platform, your documents go to Jumio, Onfido, Stripe Identity, or similar providers. They store your passport scans, selfies, and extracted personal data. One breach at their end compromises your identity forever. Your private information is no longer private.

Custodians Control Your Digital Assets

Nearly every exchange uses custodians like Fireblocks, Coinbase Custody, or BitGo. You do not own your assets, the custodian does. When policies get misconfigured or the custodian is compromised, your digital assets disappear. Not your keys, not your coins.

ByBit Custodian Hack (October 2024)

Fireblocks enabled custodian lost $1.5B+ in crypto. The attack exploited a misconfigured policy between the platform and custodian, not a direct breach of ByBit. Users who thought they had secure custody found their funds gone due to third party configuration error.

WazirX Collapse (July 2024)

$230M+ lost after a custodial wallet breach. WazirX used Liminal Custody, yet another third party in the chain. The attack exploited multisig wallet configuration vulnerabilities. Users funds were stolen through no fault of their own.

The Sub-Processing Chain Never Ends

KYC providers and custodians themselves use cloud services (AWS, GCP), ML APIs, support contractors, and data analytics firms. Your personal information and private keys propagate through an expanding attack surface. Every link is a potential breach point for both your identity and your digital assets.

Distributed Architecture

Two critical purposes: distributed secret storage AND fault tolerance

1 Distributed Secret Storage

Shamir's Secret Sharing (2 of 3 threshold) ensures no single server ever has the complete private key.

Shamir Secret Sharing (2 of 3 threshold)

  • Client splits secret into 3 shares
  • Each share sent to independent server
  • No single server has complete secret
  • Any 2 shares can reconstruct the original
  • Loss of 1 server = no data loss

2 Fault Tolerance & High Availability

Instances can accept jobs on behalf of each other. When a down instance recovers, it processes queued jobs without client resubmission.

Fault Tolerance & High Availability

  • Instances monitor each other's health via RethinkDB real-time sync
  • Jobs are queued for any instance that is down
  • No client retry needed, queued jobs process automatically on recovery
  • 24/7 availability, system functions with any 2 of 3 instances

Combined: Security + Reliability

How Do You Recover Lost Keys Without Trusted Third Parties?

Your authentication happens via private and public keypairs derived from a randomly generated secret. This secret is stored securely client side behind biometrically secured local storage.

But what happens when your device is lost or the application is uninstalled and local storage is purged?

OSINT Background Screening

Zero API Costs. Self-Contained. Algorithmically Neutral.

4-Phase Screening Pipeline

Algorithmic background checks without expensive external APIs. Self-contained databases and open-source models deliver enterprise-grade screening at zero marginal cost.

🔍

Face-Verified Image Search

DuckDuckGo image search + DeepFace facial recognition (Facenet512). Searches for "{name} photo", "{name} profile", "{name} {country}". Downloads up to 10 images and extracts face embeddings using RetinaFace. Only analyzes articles with verified photo matches (cosine similarity ≥ 0.85). Eliminates false positives from common names. 24-hour caching with exponential backoff.

📰

NLP-Enhanced News Analysis

DuckDuckGo web search with multiple query variations. Trafilatura extracts article content (7 rotating User-Agents, rate limiting). VADER sentiment filters positive results (compound < -0.3). FinBERT classifies financial crimes: fraud, corruption, money_laundering. Event type detection: conviction (≥0.7), investigation (≥0.5), allegation (≥0.4), cleared. spaCy NER ensures relevance.

🗂️

PEP & Sanctions Database

Self-contained OSSPEP database with 100K+ entries synced from OFAC, EU, UN lists. Fuzzy name matching using Jaccard similarity + Coverage score. Binary confidence: 99% (Name+Country+DOB), 66% (Name+Country), 33% (Name only). Handles "First Last" and "Last, First" formats. ISO 2-letter country codes with 60+ variations. DOB range support (YYYY-MM-DD, YYYY-MM, YYYY). Filters by status (current/former).

⚖️

Binary PASS/FAIL Decision

Clear thresholds for compliance. FAIL if sanctions confidence ≥ 65%, PEP confidence ≥ 65%, or negative news score ≥ 50. No continuous scoring—uses binary decision model. Full audit trail with evidence URLs, face match percentages, and classification confidence scores. Deterministic and reproducible.

Identity Verification

Proof-of-Life + Proof-of-Identity + Proof-of-Address. Algorithmic verification without human review.

Three-Layer Verification System

NI6 performs comprehensive identity verification through three complementary layers: proof-of-life ensures the person is real and present, proof-of-identity validates that documents belong to the same person and are current, and proof-of-address verifies the residential address from the bank statement.

1

Proof-of-Life Verification

👤
Video Selfie Authentication

Ensures the applicant is a real, live person physically present during verification—preventing deepfakes, photos of photos, and replay attacks.

  • Hand Gesture Detection: MediaPipe tracks 21 hand landmarks per finger. Applicant displays specific gestures at exact timestamps (e.g., show "4" fingers at 4.5s, "3" fingers at 8.5s).
  • Liveness Detection: DeepFace anti-spoofing models detect presentation attacks (printed photos, masks, screen replays).
  • Temporal Consistency: Video analysis ensures natural motion and timing—AI-generated videos cannot match real human dynamics.
  • Anti-Replay: Each video is unique. Time-based gesture sequences cannot be reused or recorded for later submission.
2

Proof-of-Identity Verification

📄
Document Cross-Validation

Validates that all documents belong to the same person and are legally valid for identity verification.

  • Face Matching: Passport photo face embedding compared with selfie face embedding using Facenet512. Cosine similarity threshold ensures the same person in both documents.
  • Name Cross-Check: Name extracted from bank statement compared with name on passport using fuzzy string matching. Detects discrepancies, maiden names, or typos.
  • Passport Expiration: Passport expiration date extracted and validated against current date. Expired passports automatically rejected.
  • Bank Statement Recency: Bank statement date checked to ensure document is recent enough (configurable threshold, typically 3 months). Prevents using old addresses or outdated information.
3

Proof-of-Address Verification

🏠
Bank Statement Address Validation

Extracts and validates the residential address from the bank statement to ensure the applicant has a verifiable address and the document is authentic.

  • Address Extraction: DoTR OCR extracts the full residential address from the bank statement including street address, city, state/province, postal code, and country.
  • Format Validation: Validates address format matches expected patterns for the declared country. Detects malformed or incomplete addresses.
  • Document Authenticity: Bank statement metadata, logo detection, and layout analysis verify the document is a genuine bank statement—not a forged or manipulated image.
  • Name-Address Consistency: Ensures the name on the bank statement matches the extracted address recipient name, preventing use of another person's statement.
  • PO Box Detection: Optionally flags PO Box addresses based on compliance requirements. Some jurisdictions require physical residential addresses.
  • Country Validation: Validates that the address country matches the passport issuing country or declared country of residence.

Why Three-Layer Verification?

🎯

Prevents Identity Fraud

Face matching and name cross-validation ensure documents belong to the same person. Cannot use stolen documents with different names.

⏰

Ensures Currency

Passport expiration and bank statement recency checks prevent using outdated or expired documents for verification.

🏠

Validates Proof-of-Address

Bank statement address extraction and format validation ensure the applicant has a verifiable residential address. Prevents fake or stolen addresses.

🤖

Detects Presentation Attacks

Liveness detection via video selfies and hand gestures thwarts deepfakes, photos of photos, and replay attacks.

🔐

No Human Review

Algorithmic verification eliminates human bias and ensures consistent decisions. Full audit trail for compliance.

The Superlexicon Project

Distributed systems patterns, documented

What is Superlexicon?

Superlexicon is Mano Thanabalan's project to document reusable distributed systems patterns. Think of it like a pattern library, but for decentralized infrastructure.

NI6 = Pattern #1

NI6 is the first pattern in Superlexicon. It shows how to do self-custody without trusted third parties, while still staying compliant with KYC regulations.

Where this is going

The end goal: a blockchain with zero fees. No proof-of-waste, no staking required. Just efficient distributed consensus.

The pattern approach

Each Superlexicon pattern is something you can actually reuse. NI6 combines Shamir's secret sharing, video selfie biometrics, and algorithmic KYC to remove custodians from the equation. Future patterns will tackle other distributed systems problems — consensus without burning electricity, permissionless networks without token speculation, infrastructure that doesn't collapse when the token price dumps.
